# GT racing template

Best for GT3, endurance GT, touring-car, and track-day coupe concepts.

## Template

```text
Original {vehicleClass}, {era} endurance racing design, {designLanguage}.
Long hood, cabin set rearward, short rear deck, wide track, {stance},
wheels pushed to the corners, deep-dish center-lock wheels,
front splitter with endplates, canards, vented hood, louvered fenders,
side skirts, rear diffuser, swan-neck rear wing, {aero}.
{bodyMaterial} body in {livery}, {accent} accents, no sponsor logos.
{view}. Bilateral symmetry, crisp panel gaps, hard-surface, full car visible.
{lighting}, {background}, no motion blur.
Negative: no logos, no text, no production-car badge, no mismatched wheels,
no extra wheels, no melted fenders, no cropped car.
```

## GT-specific guidance

- "Long hood, cabin rearward" is the core proportion cue.
- Wide fenders and a big rear wing should feel functional, not decorative.
- Endurance cars look good with louvered fenders and a roof scoop.
- Avoid naming a real manufacturer or model. Use era and form language instead.
- For image-to-3D, remove the wing's background clutter and keep the full car in
  frame.
