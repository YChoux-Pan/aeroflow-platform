# Cinematic racing template

Use for poster frames, hero images, and social previews. Do not use these as
3D reference images.

## Template

```text
{shot} of an original {vehicleClass}, {era}, {designLanguage}.
{stance}, {aero}, {materials}, {livery}, no logos, no text.
{scene}. {camera}. {lighting}. {background}.
Cinematic, photorealistic, high dynamic range, sharp focus on the car,
controlled depth of field, realistic reflections, no watermark.
Negative: no logos, no text, no duplicated wheels, no extra cars unless asked,
no distorted track architecture, no driver unless asked.
```

## Shot recipes

| Shot | Camera | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| Low hero | 24–35 mm, 0.4 m above the track, looking slightly up | pit lane at dawn |
| Tracking | side-on, long lens, car sharp, background streaked | wet circuit |
| Rear 3/4 | 50 mm, low, three-quarter rear | tunnel or garage |
| Details | 85–100 mm macro | front wheel, fender, wing endplate |
| Atmospheric | 35 mm, wide | dusk, mist, or light rain |

## Notes

- Keep the car original and brand-free.
- Motion blur should affect the background, not the car, unless a panning shot is
  specifically requested.
- Use practical light sources: brake glow, track lights, garage strip lights.
- Avoid asking for "a Formula 1 car" plus a real team color/livery.
