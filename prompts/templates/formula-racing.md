# Formula / open-wheel template

Best for F1-inspired, Formula E-inspired, and original open-wheel concepts.
Avoid real team liveries and current-car copies.

## Template

```text
Original modern open-wheel racing car, {era} formula concept, {designLanguage}.
Low nose, exposed wheels, halo, multi-element front and rear wings,
undercut sidepods, sculpted floor edge, exposed carbon-fiber suspension,
central shark fin, rear diffuser, {wheelSize} wheels.
Carbon-fiber primary structure with {livery}, {accent} accents, no sponsor logos.
{stance}. Bilateral symmetry, crisp panel gaps, hard-surface.
{view}. {lighting}, {background}, isolated vehicle, no motion blur.
Negative: no logos, no text, no real team identity, no sponsored livery,
no asymmetric wing elements, no extra wheels, no deformed suspension.
```

## Formula-specific guidance

- Open-wheel cars reveal errors: both front wings must match, suspension arms
  must connect plausibly to the chassis, and the rear wing must have supports.
- Say "open-wheel" explicitly; otherwise image models may add fenders.
- Halo is a strong era cue. Remove it for a classic or fictional era.
- Specify "exposed wheels" and "no fenders" if the model keeps closing the body.
- For 3D, hard edges matter more than tiny sponsor-like graphics. Ask for
  "crisp panel gaps, exposed carbon weave, symmetrical, no motion blur."
