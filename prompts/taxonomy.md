# Vehicle prompt taxonomy

Use consistent vocabulary across all generated views. A cohesive design brief
beats a long adjective list.

## Classes

| Class | Silhouette anchors | Typical aero language |
|---|---|---|
| Formula / open-wheel | exposed wheels, low nose, halo, multi-element wings | front wing, endplates, floor edge, diffuser, beam wing |
| Le Mans hypercar | closed cockpit, pronounced fenders, long tail | dive planes, gurney flaps, undercut sidepod, swan-neck wing |
| GT3 / endurance GT | long hood, wide track, big rear wing | splitter, canards, louvered fenders, rear diffuser |
| Rally | short overhangs, tall ride, compact cabin | mud flaps, roof scoop, skid plates, wide arches |
| Drift / time attack | low, wide, aggressive camber, huge wing | canards, vented hood, chassis-mounted wing, diffuser |
| Track-day coupe | street-legal silhouette, mild aero | splitter, ducktail, side skirts, rear diffuser |
| Concept hypercar | cab-forward, dramatic fender volumes | active aero, blown diffuser, aerodynamic channels |

## Proportions

- Wheel diameter / body height: sports 35–40%, supercar 40–45%,
  SUV/truck 25–30%, open-wheel can run higher.
- Wheelbase / overall length: sports and GT roughly 55–65%.
- Overhangs: short for racing cars; a long rear overhang can suggest a
  prototype or aero testing.
- Ground clearance: 50–100 mm for a low race car; 150–220 mm for rally.
- Track width: wide relative to body; wheels should visually fill the arches.

## Form language

- **Wedge**: rising beltline, low nose, rear mass. Works for 1970s and modern
  prototypes.
- **Cab-forward**: short front overhang, windscreen pushed ahead, large rear
  volume. Works for hypercars and EV concepts.
- **Long hood / short deck**: classic front-engine GT proportion.
- **Floating roof / negative surfaces**: modern performance EV language.
- **Function-first**: exposed suspension, structural wishbones, riveted panels,
  aero surfaces. Works for motorsport.

## Materials and livery

Prefer 2–3 materials and 1 accent color per design. State whether surfaces are
gloss or matte. Specify "no logos" and "no text" explicitly for reusable assets.

## View vocabulary

- `front three-quarter`: 35–45° from the nose, slightly above headlight height.
- `side profile`: true 90° profile; minimal perspective distortion.
- `rear three-quarter`: 35–45° from the rear, showing diffuser and wing.
- `top-down`: 90° orthographic-like; use for layout and design sheets.
- For 3D reference: front + side + rear three-quarter are the minimum trio.

## Rendering vocabulary

- Studio: softbox, even fill, rim light, seamless background, contact shadow.
- Cinematic: low key, practical highlights, wet asphalt, atmospheric haze.
- Technical: orthographic, dimension lines, callout arrows, wireframe overlay,
  monochrome blueprint with one accent.
- 3D reference: no dramatic lighting, no motion blur, no crop, no people.
