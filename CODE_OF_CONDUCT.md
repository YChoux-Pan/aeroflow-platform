# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want AeroFlow to be a welcoming engineering and design community regardless
of experience, identity, discipline, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific and kind in technical review.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect licenses and attribution; do not ask others to redistribute
  unlicensed assets or skills.
- Respect intellectual property: no real team liveries, manufacturer logos, or
  copied production-car geometry in contributed examples.
- Disclose when generated content or an external source influenced a
  contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private credentials or personal data.
- Submitting malware, hidden execution, or prompt-injection payloads.
- Deliberately misrepresenting a visual proxy as certified aerodynamic analysis.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this code, and
may block repeat offenders. Report concerns through the repository's private
security channel when confidentiality matters.
